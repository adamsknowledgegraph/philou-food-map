PARIS' BEST ARTISAN ADDRESSES BY PHILIPPINE DARBLAY

Philippine Darblay is a French lifestyle influencer with a passion for gastronomy, well-being, and the art of living. Through her Instagram account @philoudarblay and her video series “Les Adresses de Philippine”, she shares her favorite spots, refined discoveries, and a thoughtful take on Parisian lifestyle trends.

 "Paris is full of artisanal spots where you can sometimes see the workshops in action. Every day, with each shoot, I’m reminded of how lucky we are to have such a rich heritage. Thank you to the artists and artisans who keep it alive.


For Longchamp, I’ve selected three places to discover and support:

Tant d’Avenir, for its raw, handmade jewelry crafted entirely in Paris.

Duvelleroy, for its ability to make the fan feel exceptionally modern.

L’Entropiste, for its bold and eccentric olfactory universe."

Eloise Gilles, fan maker


Duvelleroy- Parisian, 17 Rue Amélie, 75007 Paris

Bertrand Duchaufour, perfumer


L’Entropiste, 59 Rue des Francs-Bourgeois, 75004 Paris

Quentin Pontonnier, jeweler


Tant d’Avenir, 44 Rue Albert Thomas, 75010 Paris

From Tokyo to New York, Seoul to Paris, everyone has chosen three iconic places from the heart. Some secret, some unmissable, these locations make up a geography of craftsmanship and the art of living. Much more than a simple city guide, the project gives rise to a Longchamp World Guide – an invitation to explore our planet through the Maison's eyes, sharpened to handiwork, detail and authenticity.
